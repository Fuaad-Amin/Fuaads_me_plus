About Me

Introduction

Hi, I'm Fuaad!

I'm Fuaad Amin, a recent business graduate of the University of California, Riverside. I always loved mathematics but near the end of my time at UCR I got really into formal mathematics and audited a graduate class in complex analysis.

Like every multipotentialite, my interests span several seemingly unrelated fields, including algebraic geometry, modular origami, twisty puzzles, chess, Minecraft redstone, and recently programming. What I love most about these areas are their connections to complex problem solving methods and underlying mathematical ideas

I created this website as the ultimate form of accountability. This is a place where I can publish articles, share projects, keep track of everything I'm learning along the way.

Academic Interests

Mathematics

Mathematics is my primary academic interest. While my formal university studies include business, I spend much of my independent study time exploring advanced mathematics.

I'm particularly interested in algebraic geometry. It's a field that connects abstract algebra, a field that is very near and dear to me, with the study of geometric objects. We initially look at mathematical objects called "Varieties" which are the rigid representations of zeros of polynomial equations. We then we graduate to studying Schemes, the Zariski topology, and Hilbert's Nullstellensatz. (Don't be intimidated by those words as I also have little clue what they mean)

My broader mathematical interests include Abstract Algebra, Complex Analysis, Algebraic Topology, and Category Theory.

My long-term goal is to pursue a PhD in mathematics and contribute original research to the field. Along the way, I hope to make advanced math more accessible through my notes and mathematical projects.

Where It All Started

Origami & Twisty Puzzles

Long before I decided to learn mathematics, there were two hobbies that would govern most of my interests later on. Twisty-Puzzles and modular origami.

I've spent over a decade folding paper into geometric objects, and developing some of my own designs. The process of assembling beautiful geometric structures from such simple pieces speaks volumes about the art form. Sitting down to fold the same thing over and over is a meditative process and after putting them together, you're rewarded with a stunning geometric construction.

A curse of being incredibly engrained in a hobby is poor money management. I have a large twisty puzzle collection and I have unique solutions to almost all of them. It's a very satisfying feeling solving a puzzle independently of a tutorial and coming up with a solution.

These hobbies introduced me to my love for more formal ideas in mathematics such as symmetry, transformations, polyhedra, combinatorics, and group theory.

You can find my collections, original designs, and related mathematical explorations in the Projects section.

Sharing Knowledge

Teaching & Tutoring

Teaching has been another important part of my experience. I've worked with students in mathematics and chess, both through organized instruction and private tutoring.

I like to pick at students' brains and see what best speaks to them and help students develop their own problem-solving intuition.

One of my goals for this website and my social media is to make some of the mathematics I study a bit more accessible through intuitive explanations, with a focus on many examples.

The Philosophy

Why "Me Plus"?

The name Fuaad's Me Plus was inspired by Dr. Grace Lordan's book Think Big.

The idea of a "Me Plus" is to imagine a future version of yourself who has accomplished the goals you're working toward today.

For me, that future version is an accomplished mathematician who has made meaningful contributions to mathematics, continues learning, and shares knowledge with others.

I'm not there yet, but this website is a record of the steps I'm taking to get there.
