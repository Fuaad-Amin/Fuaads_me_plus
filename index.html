Welcome

Hi, I'm Fuaad!

I'm a business student at the University of California,Riverside. I love to study graduate math in my free time

Well before I ever opened a math textbook, I fell in love with twisty puzzles and origami. I've spent over a decade folding modular, geometric origami models and developing independent solutions to more than 200+ twisty puzzles.

What started as a few innocent hobbies became my first deep exploration of group theory, symmetries, polytopes, and geometry. Now I'm independently studying Algebraic Geometry while still designing my own origami models and diving into the mathematics behind twisty puzzles.

Why "Me Plus"?

The name Fuaad's Me Plus was inspired by Dr. Grace Lordan's book Think Big, where she describes the concept of a "Me plus" as the future version of yourself who has accomplished the goals you are working toward today.

It represents the small, consistent steps you take to close the gap between who you are and who you want to become. While I'm far away from my big goals of being an accomplished mathematician, I am learning and growing every day

The purpose of this site is to show off my interests as well as keep me accountable towards my biggest dreams.

Some parts of the site are still under construction or lead to my old google site. Feel free to look around.
